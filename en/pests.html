Identification guide

Common Pests in the Dominican Republic

Tropical conditions can support year-round pest activity. These warning signs can help you decide when to request a professional inspection.

German Cockroaches

Small dark droppings around cabinets, activity at night and insects near warm appliances may indicate an infestation.

Termites

Mud tubes, discarded wings, hollow-sounding wood or fine debris near woodwork can be signs of termite activity.

Rats and Mice

Droppings, gnaw marks, scratching sounds and damaged food packages are common rodent warning signs.

Bed Bugs

Dark spots along mattress seams, shed skins and recurring bites after sleep may warrant a careful inspection.

Fleas and Ticks

Frequent pet scratching and small jumping insects around rugs, furniture or resting areas can indicate activity.

Mosquitoes and Ants

Standing water, entry gaps and accessible food sources can sustain repeated activity around homes and businesses.
